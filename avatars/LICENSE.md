## What the avatars are made of

Rowan and Mira are original fictional characters; they do not depict real
people. They are generated by `python -m tak build` from the specifications in
`<slug>/spec.json`:

- **MakeHuman Community assets, CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication): the base
  mesh and body proportion targets, skin textures, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes,
  teeth, tongue, hair, clothing and shoes from the
  [MakeHuman system assets](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html)
  pack, the ARKit face units from
  [Faceunits 01](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/faceunits01.html)
  and the viseme targets from
  [Visemes 02](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/visemes02.html).
  Rowan's skin textures (colour, normal and specular maps) are the community
  skin *skin male african middleage* by **Mindfront**
  ([source](http://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/526)), CC0, from the
  [Skins 02](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/skins02.html)
  pack. CC0 places no conditions on reuse; the MakeHuman Community and the
  skin's author are credited here as a courtesy.
- **Authored for this kit, CC BY 4.0**: material settings, the baked skin
  maps (region masks, region tints and roughness, detail normal and colour
  layers), the skin detail tiles in `../assets/detail/skin/` (generated with an
  image generator from prompts recorded by the author and processed by the
  kit, see `provenance.json` there) and the procedural detail tiles, eye and
  skin colour adjustments, mouth occlusion colours, skin-weight and garment
  corrections, the transfer of face targets to eyes, brows, lashes, teeth and
  tongue, and the specifications.
